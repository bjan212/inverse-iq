## 📝 Content Guidelines

### **Writing Style**

#### **Do:**
- ✅ Be data-driven and specific
- ✅ Use active voice
- ✅ Show confidence in the AI
- ✅ Explain technical concepts clearly
- ✅ Include numbers and statistics

#### **Don't:**
- ❌ Guarantee profits
- ❌ Use excessive hype
- ❌ Make unrealistic claims
- ❌ Downplay risks
- ❌ Use jargon without explanation

### **Example Content**

#### **Good:**
```
"8 traders lost $45,000 going LONG in similar conditions. 
Our AI identified this pattern with 92% confidence. 
Suggested direction: SHORT"
```

#### **Avoid:**
```
"You'll definitely make money with this signal! 
Never lose again! Secret strategy revealed!"
```
