## Additional Design Notes

### Visual Elements to Include Throughout:
1. **Screenshots**: Use actual screenshots from signal-performance.html and trade-analyzer.html
2. **Charts & Graphs**: Line charts for performance, bar charts for comparisons, pie charts for market share
3. **Icons**: Modern, flat design icons for features and benefits
4. **Animations**: Subtle transitions between slides, animated charts
5. **Color Coding**: Green for positive metrics, red for risks, blue for neutral information
6. **Data Visualizations**: Real-time looking dashboards, live metrics displays

### Typography Guidelines:
- **Headings**: Bold, 36-48pt, Montserrat
- **Subheadings**: Semi-bold, 24-30pt, Montserrat
- **Body Text**: Regular, 16-20pt, Inter
- **Captions**: Light, 12-14pt, Inter

### Branding Elements:
- **Logo**: Circuit board pattern forming "X" shape
- **Tagline**: Always include "Transparent. Accountable. Profitable."
- **Color Palette**:
  - Primary: #00D4FF (Electric Blue)
  - Secondary: #00FF88 (Neon Green)
  - Accent: #6B46C1 (Deep Purple)
  - Background: #0A0E27 (Dark Navy)
  - Text: #F3F4F6 (Light Gray)
