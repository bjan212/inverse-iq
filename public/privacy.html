Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 1, 2025

Your Privacy Matters: This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data when you use Xrypt for research purposes.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Trading Data

When you submit your trading data for research purposes, we collect:

  • Trade History: Entry prices, exit prices, timestamps, profit/loss amounts
  • Market Data: Trading pairs, volumes, order types
  • Performance Metrics: Win rates, average returns, drawdowns
  • Exchange Information: Which exchange(s) you trade on

1.2 Account Information

  • API Keys: Read-only access to fetch your trading history
  • Wallet Address: For potential compensation payments
  • Email Address: Optional, for communication about your submission

1.3 Technical Data

  • IP address (for security purposes)
  • Browser type and version
  • Submission timestamps
  • Usage statistics

2. How We Use Your Information

2.1 Research & Analysis

Your data is used exclusively for research purposes:

  • Pattern Recognition: Identifying common trading patterns and behaviors
  • Market Analysis: Understanding cryptocurrency market dynamics
  • Signal Generation: Creating inverse signals based on aggregated failure patterns
  • Academic Research: Contributing to understanding of trading psychology and market efficiency

2.2 Service Improvement

  • Improving our algorithms and analysis methods
  • Enhancing signal accuracy
  • Developing new research insights

2.3 Communication

If you provide an email address, we may use it to:

  • Confirm receipt of your submission
  • Notify you about compensation
  • Request additional information if needed
  • Share research findings (optional)

3. Data Anonymization & Aggregation

Your Privacy is Protected:

All trading data is anonymized before analysis. We use the following methods:

3.1 Anonymization Process

  • No Personal Identifiers: Your name, email, and wallet address are separated from trading data
  • Unique IDs: Each submission gets a random, non-traceable identifier
  • Aggregation: Individual trades are combined with others to identify patterns
  • No Individual Exposure: Your specific trades are never displayed publicly

3.2 What We Share

Data Type Shared Publicly? Details
Individual Trades ❌ NO Never shared or displayed
Aggregated Patterns ✅ YES Combined data from multiple traders
API Keys ❌ NO Encrypted, never exposed
Wallet Address ❌ NO Used only for payments
Email ❌ NO Used only for communication
Research Insights ✅ YES General findings, no individual data

4. Data Security

4.1 Security Measures

We implement multiple layers of security:

  • Encryption: All API keys are encrypted at rest and in transit
  • HTTPS: Secure connections for all data transmission
  • Access Control: Limited access to sensitive data
  • Regular Audits: Periodic security reviews
  • Secure Storage: Data stored on secure servers

4.2 API Key Security

Your API keys are handled with extreme care:

  • Encrypted using industry-standard algorithms
  • Used only for read-only data collection
  • Never logged or displayed
  • Automatically deleted after data collection (optional)
  • Can be revoked by you at any time

5. Data Retention

5.1 How Long We Keep Your Data

  • Trading Data: Retained indefinitely for research purposes (anonymized)
  • API Keys: Can be deleted immediately after collection or retained (encrypted) for updates
  • Contact Information: Retained until you request deletion
  • Technical Logs: 90 days

5.2 Data Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of your data:

  • Contact us to request deletion
  • We will delete your personal information within 30 days
  • Anonymized, aggregated data may be retained for research
  • API keys can be deleted immediately upon request

6. Third-Party Services

6.1 Exchange APIs

We connect to cryptocurrency exchanges to collect your trading data:

  • Binance, Bybit, OKX, MEXC, and others
  • We use read-only API access
  • Subject to each exchange's privacy policy

6.2 No Data Selling

We NEVER sell your data to third parties.

Your data is used exclusively for our research purposes and is not shared with, sold to, or traded with any external companies or individuals.

7. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

7.1 Access

  • Request a copy of your submitted data
  • View what information we have about you

7.2 Correction

  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Update your contact information

7.3 Deletion

  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Remove your API keys from our system

7.4 Opt-Out

  • Stop receiving communications
  • Withdraw consent for data processing

8. Cookies & Tracking

8.1 What We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Required for the platform to function
  • Analytics: To understand how users interact with the platform
  • No Advertising Cookies: We don't use cookies for advertising

8.2 Your Control

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling cookies may affect platform functionality.

9. Children's Privacy

Xrypt is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in different countries. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for international transfers.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by:

  • Updating the "Last Updated" date
  • Sending an email (if you provided one)
  • Displaying a notice on the platform

12. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions, data deletion requests, or to exercise your rights:

  • Use the contact form on our platform
  • We will respond within 30 days

13. Research Ethics

Our research follows ethical guidelines:

  • Transparency: We're clear about how we use your data
  • Consent: You explicitly agree to participate
  • Anonymity: Your identity is protected
  • Benefit: Research aims to improve trading outcomes
  • No Harm: We don't use data in ways that could harm you
Summary:

We collect trading data for research purposes only. Your data is anonymized, encrypted, and never sold. You can request deletion anytime. We use industry-standard security. Your API keys are encrypted and used only for read-only access.
