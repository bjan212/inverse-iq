Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

Is this safe? Can you steal my funds?
Absolutely safe! We only require read-only API access, which means we can ONLY view your trading history. We cannot withdraw funds, place trades, or access your capital in any way. Your money is 100% secure.
What happens to my data?
Your trading data is anonymized and used to train AI trading algorithms. Your identity is never stored or shared. We're GDPR compliant and take data privacy seriously.
How long does payment take?
Payment is processed automatically within 5 minutes of successful validation. You'll receive USDT directly to your wallet and can track the transaction on blockchain.
Can I submit multiple accounts?
Yes! Each account is validated separately. If you have multiple trading accounts, you can submit them all and get paid for each one.
What if I don't meet the requirements?
No problem! Continue trading to build more history, then resubmit when you meet the criteria. There's no penalty for failed validation.
Which exchanges do you support?
Currently: Binance Futures, Bybit Futures, OKX Futures, and MEXC Futures. We're adding Bitget and Gate.io soon!
Do you support spot trading?
Not currently. We only collect futures trading data. Spot trading support is planned for Q1 2025.
Can I delete my data later?
Yes! Contact us with your submission ID and we'll delete your data within 30 days, as per GDPR requirements.
